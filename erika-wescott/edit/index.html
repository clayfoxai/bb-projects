Welcome to your dashboard

A quick tour of everything you can do here. About a minute.

Your menu (left). Move between Home, Site Editor, Search & Sharing, Analytics, Bookings, Testimonials, Blog, and FAQs. On a phone it sits across the top.

Home. Your at-a-glance view: visits, apply clicks, and your next calls.

Site Editor. Click any field, then Preview to try it on the right. Or click an element in the preview to jump straight to it. Save draft keeps it private; Publish is the only step that updates your live site. Revert undoes a change.

Search & Sharing. Controls how your site looks in Google, AI search, and link previews (LinkedIn, text). Includes your share image.

Analytics. How many people visit, where they come from, and how many take action, updated live.

Testimonials, Blog & FAQs. Add, edit, delete, and reorder with the ↑/↓ arrows. The Visible toggle controls what shows on your site.

Your image library

Pick an image you uploaded before, or close and upload a new one.
