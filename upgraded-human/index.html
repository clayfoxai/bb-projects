What actually happens during a visit? +
It starts with a consultation and bloodwork so your protocol is built around your actual results, not a generic stack. Treatment itself happens in-clinic, same day, and your practitioner walks you through every step.
How soon will I notice a difference? +
It varies by person and protocol. Some clients notice changes in the first few weeks, but a full response can take several months as your body does the actual work of repairing itself. We'll set clear expectations for your specific protocol during your consultation.
Is this safe? +
Every protocol is delivered under physician-led medical oversight, with sourcing held to current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) standards and third-party batch testing. As with any medical decision, your practitioner will walk you through the details and answer every question during your consultation before anything is decided.
How is this different from the HRT clinic near me? +
Most HRT storefronts offer a single service. RegenX combines diagnostics, regenerative therapy, and orthopedic care (through our partnership with Dr. Johnny East) under one roof, with one point of contact managing your whole protocol.
Why not just fly overseas, it's cheaper? +
Overseas clinics can look cheaper up front, but you're trading U.S. licensing, oversight, and accountability for it, on top of the flight and recovery travel itself. RegenX gives you the same caliber of protocol fifteen minutes from Dallas Love Field and twenty minutes from DFW Airport, with a U.S.-licensed clinic behind it.
What does a protocol cost, and is financing available? +
Investment varies by protocol and is confirmed during your consultation, since it's built around your specific goals. Ask about payment options when you book your assessment.
What if I don't notice a difference? +
Your practitioner stays involved after treatment and will adjust your protocol as needed. Bring any concerns to your follow-up so we can address them directly.
