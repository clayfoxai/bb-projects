Welcome to your dashboard

A quick tour of everything you can do here. It takes about a minute.

1
Your menu. Use the menu on the left — Home, Site Editor, Analytics, and Events — to move between areas. On a phone it sits across the top.
2
Home. Your at-a-glance view: how many people are visiting, how many have reached out, plus quick links to everything.
3
Site Editor — edit your website. Click any text box or photo, then Preview to try it. Save draft keeps it privately (drafts glow amber). Publish is the only step that updates your live website.
4
Analytics. See how many people visit your site, where they come from, and how many book a call or send a message — updated live.
5
Events. Add your speaking engagements and public events. Mark one “Show on site” and it appears on your website automatically. Your Events section stays hidden until you add one.
6
