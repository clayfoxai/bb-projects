Welcome to your site editor

A quick tour. It takes about a minute.

1
Make a change. Click any text box or photo on the left. You can also click a section in the live preview on the right to jump straight to it.
2
Preview it. Click Preview under a field to see your change in the preview on the right. Only you can see it.
3
Save it as a draft. Click Save draft to keep a change privately. Drafts glow amber and wait there. Your public site does not change yet.
4
Publish when you are ready. Click Publish in the top bar and confirm. This is the only step that updates your live website.
5
Undo anytime. Use Revert on a single field, or Revert all to clear every unpublished draft.
Photos are resized and optimized for you on upload, so you can use images straight from your phone. Every image you upload is saved to your library, so you can reuse it on any field with “Choose from library.” For video, add a YouTube or Vimeo link or upload a file, then choose which one plays.
Reopen this anytime from “How to use” in the top bar.
