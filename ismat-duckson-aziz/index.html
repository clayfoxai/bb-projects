My Story

Ismat Duckson Aziz is a Human Capital and C-Suite professional who started her career in Canada. Ismat has served in multiple C-Suite roles including CHRO, CAO, Chief Privacy Officer, and Chief Corporate Social Responsibility Officer. As a Canadian and US Citizen, Ismat has held public company roles in both countries. Ismat and her family are deeply involved with philanthropic causes through their family foundation.

Our family foundation supports causes related to healthcare, education, food insecurity, and programs that support veterans and their families.
